Notes:
- Oracle (0.80-0.85 both regimes) confirms the environment/policy/
  calibration pipeline is correct -- privileged ground-truth access nearly
  always predicts correctly, independent of alpha, as expected for an
  upper-bound baseline.
- Decision-Aware (0.050 both regimes) does not yet separate from the naive
  baselines at this scale -- expected, not a regression: certified conflict
  detection requires multiple repeated observations of both contested
  actions on both compared micro-contexts before it can fire (a real,
  independently-verified property of the confidence-bound construction),
  and T=20/K=4/8 topics gives each topic only ~2-3 exposures. The real test
  of whether certified splitting earns its keep is Stage 4's T=400 grid.
- Per-step latency: Oracle and Decision-Aware held flat (~1.0-1.2s/step
  throughout); Random-K, Recency, and Semantic-RAG grew steadily slower
  (~0.85s/it to ~2.1-2.2s/it) as their memory_context prompt length grew
  with accumulated precedents. Decision-Aware's bounded-summary retrieve()
  design (MAX_SLOT_CONTENT_CHARS=200) keeps its prompt length flat despite
  accumulating history -- a real GPU-timed confirmation of the design,
  matching unit-test expectations. Worth budgeting for in Stage 4/5's
  wall-clock projection.
